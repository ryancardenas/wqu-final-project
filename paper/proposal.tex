\section{Methodology}
  \label{methodology}
  To model interest rates, intraday SPX option prices provide inputs to put-call parity, resulting in a series of implied rates for each strike throughout the day. Since strikes far from the underlying price tend to exhibit higher stochastic discount factors, and since these factors are difficult to estimate without already knowing the risk-free rate, we will use only ATM and near-ATM strikes. Accounting for the small, but non-zero differences in time-to-maturity throughout the day, we arrive at a distribution of implied interest rates, the median of which will serve as our estimated risk-free rate for that day.
